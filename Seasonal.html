Favorite Fruit of Every Season

Seasonal Favorites

One fruit for each season, picked at its sweetest and ready to enjoy.

→
Spring

Strawberry

Sweet and juicy, first to ripen

Peak season: April to June

The first sweet fruit after winter. Best eaten fresh, within a day or two of picking.

  • TasteSweet, slightly tart
  • Pairs withCream, mint, balsamic
  • Good source ofVitamin C
→
Summer

Mango

Golden, tropical and sun-ripened

Peak season: May to August

Ripe when it gives slightly under your thumb and smells fragrant at the stem.

  • TasteHoney-sweet, tropical
  • Pairs withLime, chili, yogurt
  • Good source ofVitamins A and C
→
Autumn

Apple

Crisp and fresh from the orchard

Peak season: September to November

Crisp and sturdy, apples keep for weeks in a cool place, so the autumn harvest lasts into winter.

  • TasteCrisp, sweet-tart
  • Pairs withCinnamon, cheddar, oats
  • Good source ofFiber and vitamin C
→
Winter

Orange

Bright citrus for cold days

Peak season: December to February

Citrus is at its best when the days are shortest. Heavy, thin-skinned oranges are the juiciest.

  • TasteBright, sweet-tart
  • Pairs withDark chocolate, fennel
  • Good source ofVitamin C
